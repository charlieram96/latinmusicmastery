Obsidian Glass — hit choreography

One 6-second loop, three outcomes choreographed left to right: PERFECT shatters the note into shards, GOOD breaks it in half, MISS falls through the glass — turns red, vibrates, and sinks dimmed below the table. Outer lanes keep ambient flow. Watch a few loops.

In the real PixiJS build: shards get physics (gravity, spin, motion blur), the shatter gets a radial light flash, trails get additive blending, the miss gets a refraction wobble as it passes the glass, and an 88-key piano mode uses this same language with keys lighting up under the hit line. This sketch is just the choreography.
