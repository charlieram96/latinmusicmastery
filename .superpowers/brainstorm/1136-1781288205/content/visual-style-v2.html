Which art direction for the new rhythm highway?

All three keep the warm Latin Music Mastery palette (amber / terracotta / gold) and the Rock Band feel — they differ in mood and texture. Click the one that feels right. These are rough mood mockups, not final renders.

A — Neon Cantina

Late-night Latin club energy. Smoky black stage, hot amber neon rails and receptors, glossy glass note pucks with strong bloom, glassmorphism HUD chips, subtle film grain. The most "arcade Rock Band" of the three — high contrast, reads instantly.

B — Golden Hour Stadium

B — Golden Hour Stadium

Cinematic concert stage at sunset. Volumetric light beams, warm haze at the horizon, drifting embers, softer organic glow instead of hard neon. Feels like standing on stage at an open-air show. The most atmospheric and "MasterClass premium" of the three.
